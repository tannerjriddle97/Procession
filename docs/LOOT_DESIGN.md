## Brief, assumptions, and boundaries

Make each dangerous approach worthwhile, make finishing materially better than taking one easy target, and give a harder heavy storm durable rewards that improve later combat and exploration. Preserve every encounter invariant in [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md). No reward changes chanter health/healing, defender tiers/cap/pause/pacing, the encounter's emitted stability multipliers, or last-death storm termination. Earned equipment can now improve the player's damage, blocking, or personal stability retention; these proposed equipment effects are described explicitly below.

Target audience: a prepared late-copper/bronze survivor onward, with armor, healing, and a serviceable melee weapon. This is a balance assumption, **not an entry restriction or verified difficulty rating**; any-tier defenders may make early encounters much harder. A successful event can now yield an iron-equivalent specialist weapon/shield or a useful equipped neck relic. That finished-equipment alternative to crafting is intentional. No steel extraction, finished advanced machinery, unrestricted repairs, class-exclusive recipes, teleportation, permanent unequipped character bonuses, or new equipment slots enter the payout.

One world-shared event per heavy storm while allowed, currently 100% baseline encounter chance in that mode, is the accounting assumption. Multiplayer encounter scope itself remains unapproved. Use completed heavy events as the time unit: actual storm frequency, heavy-storm fraction, player success rate, world settings, and time spent suppressed have not been measured. Illustrative calendar conversion below assumes the event stays allowed and is not a vanilla frequency claim.

Rewards attach to the **visual identities** below. Bound/shiver, Veiled/bowtorn, and Resonant/locust remain proposed assignments; Hollow's bell/tuning-fork identity is explicit. A future family reassignment need not move the visual reward package.

The accepted lost-rescue premise supports cloth stays, maintenance salvage, and recovery supplies. Their survival on the present forms is our original interpretation. No item establishes four identified human souls, a conscious Chamber, or the origin of all storms. [Lore sources](LORE_RESEARCH.md), especially L8–L10 and L16, support recording/rescue technology; they do not establish these relics' existence or effects.

## What mostly vanilla means here

The **guaranteed supply/economic foundation** remains ordinary currency, a temporal gear, cloth/twine, clean bandages, mechanical salvage, and fat. None requires a novel fragment, exchange vendor, collection set, or unique-item roll to become useful. V5 adds one independently rolled **15% signature drop per eligible chanter death**, tied to that figure, independently of its ordinary rewards and the completion dressing/20% lamp. Every victory continues to pay vanilla resources after the equipment is collected. There is no first-world guarantee or completion power-item pool.

Here, mostly vanilla means the repeatable supply economy and crafting/repair inputs remain vanilla, with infrequent finished power rewards rather than routine novel components. **It does not mean the new items must have less practical value than the vanilla items.** A signature may be the most valuable part of an early event, intentionally following the user's stronger-power direction. We cannot honestly assign a percentage of subjective practical value to assert a vanilla majority. Routine payouts have independent use; the four source-specific equipment finds supply the progression chase. This is one signature per chanter, **not a four-piece armor set**: a bow, melee tool/weapon, shield and neck relic, without collection bonuses or fragments. Here “miniboss” describes a reward-bearing encounter target; the chanters' approved passive behavior is unchanged.

With the bell killed, a full event guarantees **13 rusty gears and one temporal gear**, plus the supplies in the totals table. Expected rusty gears are 14.5. Installed treasure-hunter selling stock lists a temporal gear at average price 5 rusty gears [S7]; this gives a rough **18 rusty gears of currency plus avoided purchase cost at minimum, 19.5 in expectation**, before valuing supplies. This is a purchase-cost anchor, not a sale offer, guaranteed stock, market price, or universal monetary valuation. Dressing and lamp have no proposed trader purchase price. No precise currency value is assigned to saved farming or healing time.

Vanilla comparisons [S2–S3] show why this should reward active completion rather than endless guard kills: gearfoot harvest alone declares temporal gear average 1 and Jonas randomizer average 0.4, while a sawblade locust declares Jonas randomizer average 1. The proposed whole event offers one predictable temporal gear but **zero Jonas randomizers**. Eliminating those repeated rolls matters more than reducing trivial fibers.

## Exact loot tables

All codes in this section are **vanilla `game:` codes**. `item` versus `block` is significant. Unique working names below have **no registered mod identifiers**. Proposed roll rules are explicit Bernoulli/uniform draws, not an assertion that vanilla `avg/var` has identical rounding semantics. All proposed quantities are fixed final quantities: no rusty-gear/harvest/luck/class/player-count multiplier applies to these event rewards.

Every eligible chanter death grants its guaranteed row once, plus exactly two independent rolls: **25% for extra rusty gears** (on success one or two, equally likely), and **15% for exactly one of that chanter's fixed signature item**. Each failure adds nothing. The four source rolls are independent of one another, gear rolls and the completion lamp roll. No shared random-signature selection, replacement, first-kill guarantee, pity, duplicate protection, or hidden roll. Save the result of a failed roll as well as a successful one; harvesting/reload cannot reroll. Signature drops are immediately earned by their eligible source death, even if the event is later abandoned or completion fails. They drop usable at full durability where relevant, without random quality/stats. Every signature is shared physical loot, not a copy per player.

### Completion — additional, once per legitimate victory

| Pool | Rolls and exact payout | Expected quantity per eligible victory |
|---|---|---:|
| Guaranteed | Once: 4 `item game:gear-rusty`, 1 `item game:gear-temporal`, 1 Rescue dressing | 4 / 1 / 1 respectively |
| Rare utility/trophy | Once: 20% one Listener's lamp; 80% nothing | 0.2 lamps |

Completion adds no power-signature roll and never duplicates a signature already rolled by a chanter. The final chanter can roll its own signature exactly once like the other three. A full eligible clear can therefore yield **zero to four signatures**, one of each at most from that event; the same types may recur across different storms. No world-first or player-first tracking is needed for this pool.

The last chanter receives **both its own ordinary/signature rolls and this separate completion payout**. No guard/bell kill, note discovery, ritual step, chest hunt, or extra fight is required. The bell can survive a valid victory. Completion is world-shared, not one copy per participant. Repeat lamps and signatures are allowed across storms, with no automatic replacement by dressing/gears; duplicates can be gifts or spare equipment. No crafting fragments are involved. Taking a wanted signature and retreating is a legitimate partial reward; finishing still guarantees the temporal gear, four additional rusty gears and dressing, with its independent lamp chance.

### Defenders and the opening bell

| Origin | Material policy | Stability recovery | Rolls / expected amounts |
|---|---|---|---|
| Initial four counted guards | No drops or harvest yield | 0 | Zero rolls / zero |
| Every replenishment, including bell children and their event-owned descendants | Same, including after the event ends | 0 | Zero rolls / zero |
| Single opening bell | Replace vanilla drops with 1 `item game:gear-rusty`, 1 `block game:metal-parts`, 2 `block game:metal-scraps`, once on eligible death | 0 | Guaranteed fixed quantities, no optional rolls |
| All four chanters | Their tables only | 0 | No extra stability reward |
| Independently spawned vanilla creatures | No proposed changes | Ordinary behavior | Excluded from all calculated totals |

The finite bell remains worth killing for salvage as well as combat control, but cannot supply a cylinder/Jonas jackpot on top of the proposed tables. Zero recovery for all event-owned entities is deliberately simple and closes the gearfoot stability loop. This **recommends changing event-specific drops/recovery**, still awaiting approval; it is not a claim that vanilla enemies currently behave this way. Defender kills retain the agreed shared reinforcement pause and tactical value. Do not alter their tiers, health, spawn rate, or healing to compensate.

Suppress every reward route: direct death drops, knife harvest, rip harvest, randomizers, and any transformed variant's rewards. Retain provenance after luring, unloading, reload, surviving the victory, or unexpected transformations. Do not globally patch ordinary mobs. This bounds **additional event loot**, not all possible storm farming: ordinary storm monsters remain outside the cap and can still be farmed during an extended storm. Whether that existing-storm incentive is acceptable remains a user/playtest question; no timer or win-condition change is proposed here.

Recommend refusing hacking/capture of tagged event guards, with explicit feedback and **no tool durability, ingredient, charge, or interaction consumption** on refusal. Otherwise replenishment could export free permanent helpers or carried creatures. This is a proposed interaction restriction, not an approved combat rule. Unexpected third-party transformations must retain provenance/reward suppression; compatibility is untested. If that restriction cannot be implemented without changing normal combat or ordinary creatures, resolve it before shipping rather than silently allowing export.

## Unique specifications — six total, including four chanter signatures

### U1 — Rescue dressing

**Appearance/purpose/source:** a short roll of dark linen stays, waxed edges and an oxidized clasp. One guaranteed per eligible completion, shared among the party. Recovery supplies left in the failed apparatus give the Bound/shared rescue work a tangible reward; the name and recovered material are original additions, not proof the figures were medics or carried human possessions.

**Exact proposed effect:** self-use only. Hold use for **2 uninterrupted real seconds**. Moving on the ground is allowed; taking damage, jumping, swimming, switching the active item, releasing early, death, or disconnect cancels the application without consuming it. Fail free at full health, during an existing vanilla healing-item effect, during an existing dressing effect, or during its cooldown. Missing fewer than six HP is allowed, with overheal lost.

On server-confirmed success, consume **one** dressing and heal **1 HP at each of +2, +4, +6, +8, +10, +12 real seconds**, for **6 HP over 12 seconds**, no initial burst. Character stats are unchanged. Effect range is self (0 blocks); no ally targeting, area effect, stability recovery, maximum-health increase, damage reduction, or resurrection. Switching items after successful application does not cancel the ticks; later ordinary damage does not cancel those ticks. Death cancels remaining healing without refund.

**Stacking/activation:** one dressing effect per player. A **15-real-second personal cooldown** begins on successful application, shared across all copies, persists through death/relog, and elapses with authoritative server time while the world runs. No duplicate stacking, refresh, paused-storage exploit, or cooldown reset by item transfer. Server shutdown pauses the timer. Starting a vanilla healing-item application while dressing ticks remain cancels the remaining dressing healing immediately, without refund; that vanilla application proceeds normally, even if subsequently canceled. This gives vanilla healing priority instead of denying an emergency input. A dressing cannot start while any vanilla healing-item effect remains. Natural regeneration/food is unaffected. Cross-mod healers need a compatibility audit; no blanket control of unknown mod healing is promised.

On intentional player logout or network disconnect, remaining scheduled ticks are lost without refund; cooldown persists. **Server/world shutdown is a persistence boundary rather than a disconnect cancellation:** preserve pending tick offsets and cooldown, pause authoritative time, and resume those future offsets once on restart. Save/reload must never replay already awarded ticks. Stack size **8**; one charge per item; no durability, repair, recharge, crafting, salvage, aging transition, or trader price. It can be stored and transferred normally. The lamp and dressing have no set bonus.

**Verified comparison / tradeoff:** installed clean bandage: 3 HP, 3-second application, 10-second effect; linen-honey-sulfur poultice: 7 HP, 3-second application, 10-second effect [S4]. Dressing provides **shorter activation exposure**, not faster total recovery: 14 seconds from activation start through last tick, versus the poultice's declared 13 seconds. It heals less than the best poultice, has a cooldown, and cannot be manufactured in bulk. Two existing clean bandages in Bound's payout also total 6 nominal HP, with two applications. Existing medicine stays relevant.

**Acquisition/repeat value/scope:** first successful event onward, no new progression gate; one useful emergency consumable each victory. No rare plant, alcohol, or sulfur supply is generated by converting it. Estimated **modest custom behavior**, with material compatibility risk: authoritative application/ticks, vanilla-priority cancellation, persisted cooldown, and UI feedback. Assets alone cannot be assumed to enforce the healing rules. No feasibility or runtime test claimed.

### U2 — Listener's lamp

**Appearance/purpose/source:** a tabletop-sized tarnished U-shaped frame around a frosted teal core; a salvaged echo of the listener's open fork. One lamp on the independent 20% completion roll. It is a working light and a recognizable victory furnishing. Salvaged light within rescue equipment is original mod fiction, not an assertion that canonical bells use this light or that the Chamber powers it.

**Exact proposed effect:** constant light HSV **[28, 2, 16]** while switched on, both in an active hand and when placed on a floor. Uses ordinary engine attenuation; **no fixed radius is promised**, and there is no sound, detection, healing, stability, heat, ignition, water, storm, combat, or special spawn/growth effect. Ordinary engine consequences of block light remain applicable; this does not grant spawn immunity. It does not clear storm fog or provide night vision. Emits light while submerged without modifying water or breathing, an original enclosed-light behavior to verify.

**Activation/limits:** default on when awarded. Complete a **0.25-real-second use** to toggle; when held, target empty space, or target the placed lamp to toggle it. Floor placement/pickup uses a separate sneak-use interaction so toggling cannot accidentally place it. No toggle cooldown beyond interaction duration. Active in either one hand, occupying that hand; inventory storage emits no light. No wearable slot, pet/vehicle mounting, remote activation, beam mode, or aura. Multiple lamps give only normal overlapping block light, with no additive personal bonus. Stack size **1**.

Unlimited lifetime, no fuel/charges/durability/repair/recharge/crafting recipe or trader price. Breaking/picking it up returns the **same lamp once**, preserving toggle state; relocating or reloading cannot duplicate it. No metal extraction, crafting ingredient use, or conversion into a vanilla lantern. Ordinary loss/destruction rules still apply: unlimited lifetime is not indestructibility. Range is engine lighting only; no gameplay effect duration other than being switched on.

**Verified comparison / tradeoff:** installed oil lamp V11, basic torch V14, small lantern V18, large lantern V20 [S5]. This light is a convenient permanent middle option with a distinctive shape/color, **dimmer than both standard lantern sizes**; it is not superior night vision or a new armor tier. Standard lantern recipe still requires ordinary plate, strips, clear quartz/glass, and candle [S5]; winning an event can substitute for making a weaker light, an intentional small convenience reward rather than a metalworking unlock. Its underwater behavior is a proposed advantage over an open flame, not a claim that ordinary lanterns fail underwater.

**Acquisition/repeat value/scope:** available after an early prepared victory, no claim of gated technology. Duplicate copies furnish paths/homes and are transferable gifts, but the economic repeat motive is primarily the vanilla payout plus dressing. Estimated **modest custom behavior** for one persisted toggle across held/placed forms, light updates, and lossless pickup; ordinary models/textures/localization also required. No unrelated machinery/fuel system. Engine held-light and underwater details require later verification.

### U3 — Breach hook

**Appearance, purpose, and source:** a short iron-colored cutting hook with a forked reinforcing rib, frayed linen grip, and a thin teal seam. **15% from the Resonant only.** A recovered cutter/lever from its mechanical-maintenance assembly, adapted as a close weapon; its bracing and cage-like reinforcement echo the tender silhouette. Salvage does not imply the chanter wielded it. It lets a storm survivor fight the answering creatures more effectively. Rescue tools becoming useful salvage is original lore; no canon source identifies this weapon, its enchantment, or its maker. Passive chanters stay passive; the Resonant/locust family assignment remains proposed.

**Exact power:** one-handed melee weapon, **5 base attack power**, **2.5-block reach**, **tool tier 4**, normal iron-falx attack timing and damage-tier handling. No reach, speed, armor penetration or stagger upgrade beyond that reference. Against the exact hostile variants listed below, multiply this weapon's **base attack contribution by 1.25**, giving **6.25 before ordinary player modifiers and target armor/tier processing**. Apply ordinary player damage modifiers once afterward. Do not multiply other equipped weapons, flat on-hit effects, final health loss, thorns, damage-over-time, projectiles or another player's attack. No second bonus trigger, cleave, ranged effect, aura or cooldown.

Allowlist (all `game:`): `drifter-normal`, `drifter-deep`, `drifter-tainted`, `drifter-corrupt`, `drifter-nightmare`, `drifter-double-headed`; `bowtorn-surface`, `bowtorn-deep`, `bowtorn-tainted`, `bowtorn-corrupt`, `bowtorn-nightmare`, `bowtorn-gearfoot`; `shiver-surface`, `shiver-deep`, `shiver-tainted`, `shiver-corrupt`, `shiver-nightmare`, `shiver-stilt`, `shiver-bellhead`, `shiver-deepsplit`; `locust-bronze`, `locust-corrupt`, `locust-corrupt-sawblade`; `bell-normal`; and the four eventual registered mod chanter types. Explicitly exclude player-owned, tamed, hacked, or friendly ordinary targets, even if their base code matches. **The four passive chanters are explicitly eligible despite being nonattacking**; do not use an aggression check to exclude them. No generic temporal/mechanical tag expands this list to bosses, livestock, wolves, other mods, `bellmini`, or all entities spawned during a storm. Against everything else it deals the ordinary 5 base attack contribution.

**Use, durability and repair:** ordinary melee activation; no charging, charges, duration, or active cooldown beyond iron-falx attack timing. **850 maximum durability**, losing exactly **1 per landed melee attack**, whether reduced by armor or not; no loss on misses, idle ticks or canceled attacks. It is a weapon, not a block-breaking/harvesting tool. At zero retain one broken, inert hook with no attack or bonus. In a proposed shapeless grid recipe, that same hook plus **1 `item game:ingot-iron`** restores **250 durability**, capped at 850; consumes the ingot, returns exactly one same-identity hook, and accepts broken hooks. Do not reset other attributes, refill to maximum automatically, or increase maximum durability. No recipe without the original relic, smelting, extraction, trader price or conversion to a vanilla blade. Stack size 1. This relic does **not** accept sharpening, quenching, or item-buff recipes; ordinary character damage modifiers still apply. The same prohibition must hold after repair.

**Comparison, acquisition and tradeoff:** installed iron falx has base 5, tool tier 4, durability 850; steel falx base 5.25, tier 5, durability 2125 [S10]. The hook's 6.25 is **25% above iron base and about 19% above steel base against eligible foes**, but it keeps iron-level tier/durability and cannot inherit sharpening/buff benefits. This does not establish superior damage through every armor tier or superiority to a modified steel weapon. Against animals or players it remains iron base, below steel, and it cannot become steel stock. Repairs support a long-term favorite at an ordinary iron cost rather than requiring replacement relic fragments. Winning before ironworking can yield an iron-equivalent weapon; that finite alternative is intentional, not a claim the iron progression gate is untouched.

**Scope:** estimated modest custom combat/repair behavior plus ordinary assets: explicit target registry, one damage contribution multiplier, wear, broken state and identity-preserving repairs. Client/server melee, armor interaction, sharpening exclusion, mod transformations and recipe retries need later verification. No feasibility test claimed.

### U4 — Braceguard

**Appearance, purpose, and source:** a narrow iron brace-face backed by old cloth stays, with a curved support and faintly vibrating inner bars. **15% from the Bound only.** Its load-bearing stays and reinforced curve echo the hunched holder's work: something that continues taking the weight. It is a salvaged structural brace repurposed for defense, not proof the chanter wore armor or fought with a shield. Canonical foundation is ordinary shields/metalwork [S11]; the recovered form and timed reinforcement effect are original additions. Bound/shiver remains a proposed family assignment.

**Exact power:** hand-held shield using ordinary metal-shield raise controls, directional block eligibility and movement behavior. Successful blocks absorb up to **5 melee / 10 projectile damage**. Active block chances **90% melee / 100% projectile**; passive chances **20% for each**. A successful **active** block with positive residual damage after that base absorption absorbs up to **3 additional damage**, before body armor, once per **8 real seconds**. Total possible absorption on that hit is 8 melee or 13 projectile, not a percentage damage immunity. Start the cooldown only when the extra absorption actually removes positive damage; absorbing just 1 extra damage still consumes it. A weak hit already fully absorbed by the base shield does not waste the reinforcement. Failed and passive blocks do not trigger it. No damage banking or healing from unused absorption.

Uses the ordinary shield's directional cone rather than adding wider coverage; its concrete vanilla cone must be verified during implementation. No parry timing window, reflection, knockback pulse, area protection, status immunity, fall/fire/environmental defense or blocking of normally unblockable damage. Range is the normal shield interaction with incoming attacks. Boosts from two copies/hands cannot protect the same hit twice: select the one ordinary valid block, then apply this boost once. Player cooldown is shared across all Braceguards, persists through swaps, transfer, death, logout and reload; it elapses in **active-server real seconds**, unaffected by sleeping, calendar speed, or time commands. Server shutdown pauses the remaining interval; passing the item to another player does not reset that recipient's cooldown. No aura, equipment slot, fuel or charge resource.

**Durability and repair:** stack 1, maximum **500**. Proposed custom wear is exactly **1 durability per successful block, plus 1 if its reinforcement fired**, independent of how much damage was absorbed; none on failures, misses, idle or raising. This is an explicit proposal, **not a claim about vanilla shield wear**. A final block may spend its remaining 1 durability to boost, then the shield becomes broken; never negative durability or a duplicate item. At zero, retain one inert Braceguard with no active/passive protection. Shapeless repair: that same shield + **1 `item game:metalplate-iron` + 1 `item game:cloth-plain`** restores **250**, capped at 500, accepting broken shields. Consume both inputs, preserve identity and player's cooldown, return exactly one shield. No crafting without the original, arbitrary armor repairs, item extraction, recharge system or trader price.

**Comparison, power and repeat value:** installed round metal shield declares absorption 5/10 and the same block chances; base durability 450 with material gains including iron +60 and steel +120 [S11]. Blackguard shield declares 5.5 melee / 12 projectile absorption. Braceguard is about an iron metal shield's declared durability, with a meaningful intermittent save against large hits rather than permanently exceeding every shield. It can stop 3 more incoming damage on a strong block; a second attack during cooldown gets only ordinary 5/10. Shield facing, active movement cost and armor still matter. Effective lifespan cannot be inferred by comparing durability alone because wear behavior differs and is untested. An eligible Bound kill can grant this alternative to crafting a shield; repair consumes normal forged metal/cloth. Duplicates provide spares or gifts, never extra boosts.

**Scope:** estimated modest custom defensive behavior, with significant integration tests: shield selection/armor order, per-player monotonic cooldown, custom wear, broken state, feedback and repair persistence. No new combat-resource or parry subsystem; stock shield behavior alone does not implement this specification.

### U5 — Listening clasp

**Appearance, purpose, and source:** two dull metal prongs around a small freely turning insert, suspended from a dark cloth cord. **15% from the Hollow only**; a wearable salvage piece echoing the listener's reference fork, not a named human's personal locket or a fragment of a soul. Its theme is maintaining a reference while the surroundings distort. Canon supplies the existing neck clothing category and environmental stability system [S12]; the personal attenuation effect and apparatus link are original mod mechanics/lore, not a verified canonical ability of tuning forks or gear necklaces.

**Exact power:** worn in the existing **neck clothing category**, with **warmth 0** and no other armor, health, speed, hunger, vision or healing modifier. While equipped, multiply each standard **environmental stability-loss component by 0.85**, after any Procession multiplier and before summing it with unchanged positive recovery. Covered sources are geographic instability and ordinary storm/Procession environmental drain, including normal nearby-rift environmental loss through that same standard drain path. Do not reduce voluntary temporal-gear/device expenditure, damage at zero stability, scripted direct stability costs, other mods' drains without an explicit compatibility adapter, or negative side effects from equipment. Never multiply positive regeneration, transfer, or kill recovery. Event kills still yield 0 recovery under the proposed farming policy.

The Procession still emits 3x / 2x / 1.5x / 1x ordinary storm drain; a clasp wearer experiences **2.55x / 1.7x / 1.275x / 0.85x** of the unprotected ordinary-storm rate from that component. Under constant isolated drain, the same initial stability lasts **1/0.85 ≈ 1.176**, or **17.6% longer**. It is not 15 percentage points of stability restored each tick. It does not directly create positive recovery or an area of safety. With simultaneous existing positive recovery, reducing the loss can change the net balance from falling to rising (for example, loss 1 and recovery 0.9 changes from net −0.1 to +0.05). This is an intended consequence of retaining those gains unchanged; no net-drain floor is imposed. Mixed-source conditions change the time-to-empty arithmetic, so no fixed extra minutes are promised.

**Activation/duration/stacking:** passive only while actually worn in the one neck slot, begins on equip and ends immediately on removal, death/unequip, or breakage of that slot state. It affects only subsequent losses; no refund of earlier drain or stored protection. Copies in inventory, offhand, a display or another clothing slot have no effect. One wearer/one effect, no aura or party sharing, set bonus or cumulative reduction from duplicate items. No charges, durability, fuel, cooldown, repair, crafting, recharge, dismantling, extraction or trader price. Unlimited equipped lifetime is an intended durable upgrade; neck-slot competition is a minor opportunity cost, **not presented as a major balance penalty**. It does not grant any benefit while unequipped. Stack 1, normal transferable/loss rules, state recomputed on reload rather than added again.

**Comparison and stage:** installed `clothes-neck-gear-amulet-temporal` uses the neck category and small light declaration but no stability-resistance attribute in the inspected neck definition; do not invent a vanilla resist amulet [S12]. The handbook says unstable areas/storms drain stability, and describes restoring stability by safe areas, kills, or a temporal gear plus knife with a health cost [S12]. The clasp slows consumption instead of granting a free gear-sized refill or amplifying recovery. This is a meaningful permanent equipped power reward after a difficult victory, useful beyond the next storm; its 15% reduction is the primary bound. No new mandatory fuel chore. Duplicate value is gifting/backup rather than stacking character power.

**Scope:** estimated modest custom equipped-state/environmental-drain behavior, with an important source-stage integration risk: identify the real standard drain path without scaling net change, kill awards or unrelated resource use. No stock stat named for this resistance was verified or assumed. If covered drain sources cannot be isolated, stop and resolve the design rather than implement an approximate net-stability multiplier.

### U6 — Last Call bow

**Appearance, purpose, and source:** **15% from the Veiled only.** A dark split-limbed bow wrapped in weathered veil cloth, with small grille-like fittings around the string seat. A drawn string produces a low sustained note, falling quiet at release; sound is feedback, not a lure or sonic attack. The player's bow is assembled salvage from the caller's resonant frame, not a weapon the passive figure used. The additional focus before a stronger shot echoes calling and waiting for an answer. Veiled/bowtorn remains proposed; the item is tied to its visual/role, not an assertion bowtorns historically operated rescue signals. Canon foundation is ordinary bows/ammunition [S13/W4]; this form, provenance and power are original mod additions.

**Exact power and activation:** normal longbow baseline: **3.75 bow damage contribution, +0.2 ranged-accuracy modifier, damage tier 0**, ordinary longbow projectile velocity, aim/movement/cancel behavior and range/trajectory. No accuracy/spread, velocity, draw-speed or tier upgrade beyond the reference. A release that is legal under ordinary bow rules may emit a **precision shot** if this uninterrupted draw has lasted at least **2.25 real seconds** and the player's Last Call cooldown is ready. The 2.25-second threshold is a proposed exact hold time from draw start, **not a claim that vanilla fully settles its aim at that time**. Published stock code rejects release below 0.65 seconds; that is not permission to bypass other stock firing/cancel conditions [W4]. Earlier legal shots fire normally without the precision bonus.

On a precision release, multiply **(3.75 + the ordinary arrow's damage contribution) by 1.25**, then apply ordinary character ranged modifiers once and normal target defenses. Lock eligibility and that weapon/ammunition contribution on **projectile emission**, not on impact: item swapping cannot add/remove/reapply the bonus. For example, an iron arrow (+2) gives 5.75 normally or **7.1875** precision; steel (+2.5) gives 6.25 or **7.8125**, before character/armor. This is a stronger deliberate shot against any normally legal target, subject to normal PvP/friendly-fire rules. No bonus melee attack, tracking, extra target, penetration, armor-tier bypass, damage-over-time or bypass of invulnerability frames. **The chanters retain their full 50% ranged resistance**, applied normally after the shot contribution. This bow does not make ranged the intended efficient way to finish them.

**Cadence, ammo and cancellation:** a precision release starts a **6-active-server-real-second cooldown**, even if the arrow misses. Shared per player across all copies; persist through item swaps, death, logout and reload, world-running elapsed time counts, shutdown pauses. Sleeping/calendar/time commands do not accelerate it. No charges or stored shots. While cooling down the bow still fires ordinary legal shots. A cooldown that expires during an uninterrupted draw can allow precision if its 2.25-second hold requirement is also met. Switching items, cancellation, death, disconnect or shutdown clears any prepared draw; no fired projectile means no bonus cooldown, ammo or wear spent. Resuming a world does not resume a partially drawn bow.

Emit **one** ordinary-arrow projectile and consume **one** arrow on each successful release. Ordinary arrow break/recovery rules stay in force. Precision does not attach a bonus to the recoverable ammunition item; no boost survives recovery into a later shot, and impact/deflection handling cannot award the same shot bonus twice. Release must server-validate and atomically account for arrow consumption, bow wear, cooldown start and single projectile creation; retries cannot duplicate it. Normal ordinary damage interruptions/cancel rules remain applicable. No special perpetual draw or offhand shield activation is added.

**Lifetime and repairs:** stack 1, **750 maximum durability**. Custom proposed wear: 1 per emitted ordinary shot, **2 per emitted precision shot**, even on a miss; none when a draw is canceled. At 1 durability, one final precision shot is allowed, then the bow becomes one broken inert item; never negative durability. Retain that same identity for a proposed shapeless repair: original bow + **2 `item game:flaxtwine` + 1 `item game:resin`** restores **250**, capped at 750. Consume inputs, return one same-identity bow, preserve player cooldown; no crafting without the original or resetting item state. Broken bows accept repair but cannot shoot. No dismantling, smelting/extraction, trader price, sharpening/quenching/item-buff recipe or conversion to a vanilla recurve. This is a **custom proposed repair**, not a claim stock bows accept these materials. Any class can use/repair it; normal class/ranged modifiers still apply once, and no bowyer recipe or trait is unlocked.

**Power, comparison and repeat value:** installed longbow declares 3.75 damage, +0.2 accuracy and 750 durability; recurve 4, +0.3 and 600, with a bowyer crafting requirement [S13]. Last Call uses the nongated longbow baseline. With the same iron arrow its precision contribution 7.1875 is 25% above longbow 5.75, but holding 2.25 seconds and waiting six seconds between boosted releases limits sustained advantage; ordinary recurve accuracy/base damage and normal class traits retain value. Against strong targets a prepared opening shot is useful without crowd control or free ammunition. Repairable lifetime lets a rare find remain a favorite; duplicates are spares/gifts, not stronger charges. This substitutes a rare specialized weapon for some bowcraft progression, intentionally, without yielding a recurve recipe or new advanced-metal stock.

**Scope:** estimated modest custom bow/projectile/cooldown/repair behavior plus assets, with meaningful integration risk: draw feedback, authoritative release state, modifier order, projectile ownership, recovery state, hand interactions and save/reload. It reuses normal archery rather than a spell system. Actual stock firing/aim handling in the installed version and effective modded projectile behavior require later runtime verification.

### Combined-power and maintenance rules

The four signatures have **no set bonus**. Ordinary hand/equipment restrictions apply; the bow follows normal bow use rather than granting extra shield protection while drawing. A clasp does not raise weapon damage or shield uptime; the shield does not alter reinforcement pacing or enemy attacks; the hook cannot skip shared chanter healing or improve ranged damage. Each provides its stated advantage independently. Any class can use/repair them; no class-exclusive vanilla item or recipe is granted. No weapon/shield materials can be recovered by melting or trading them through a newly invented exchange. A signature's 15% rarity is not itself a justification for immunity or unlimited damage; hook/shield/clasp power values remain as in V4, with a new themed fourth weapon.

All repair recipes consume the original relic as the same logical instance, then return one updated instance. Server authority must prevent stack merging, crafting retries, simultaneous repair/claim and reload from duplicating it or resetting cooldowns. Repairs are proposed custom recipes, not a claim that vanilla iron falxes or round shields already accept these inputs. Destroyed/lost relics require a later reward or transfer; retained broken relics remain repairable. All permanent effects require their actual item equipped/held. Future tests must cover combined relics, armor, class bonuses, PvP/friendly targets, heavy storms and direct/indirect damage.

## Delivery, failure, persistence, and multiplayer recommendations

These are **proposed reward rules**, not silent resolutions of the encounter lifecycle questions in [OPEN_QUESTIONS.md](OPEN_QUESTIONS.md).

1. The server gives an encounter a persistent ID and four stable slot identities when created. One event activation per heavy-storm ID; reload, leaving, returning, reconnecting, or switching players cannot recreate slots or an already completed event. No rewards before setup completes. Every actual chanter death still performs the approved healing/storm-end behavior regardless of reward eligibility.
2. A reward-eligible death is direct player damage, a player-attributed projectile, or a lethal environmental event **within 30 real seconds of that chanter/bell last taking player damage**. Store attribution/time server-side through reload; server downtime does not refresh it. Multiple players cooperate on the same four slots. NPC/guard-only, unattended environmental, admin removal, or despawn deaths do not yield ordinary rewards. The grace window allows a recently fought target to fall or burn; a long-lived automation tag cannot farm later manifestations. This is finite-event attribution, not proven vanilla kill-credit behavior.
3. Ordinary chanter rewards are earned independently. Taking one or several targets and retreating keeps those payouts; dead slots never respawn or pay again. A legitimate completion requires **all four slot deaths eligible in that same active event**, with no replacement or absence/removal substituted for death. The bell is optional. An ineligible slot death forfeits the completion bonus, but never prevents the approved storm termination. Explain this condition in the proposed encounter/reward help text before combat; it is a usability risk to playtest.
4. Make one persisted reward record per eligible slot, one for the bell, and one separate completion record. Each slot record includes its signature roll, both success or failure, and independently rolled extra gears, before exposing loot. Signature entitlement occurs at that slot's eligible death, not at completion or harvesting. On eligible final death, determine completion and generate its separate reward record immediately, independent of later corpse harvesting. Record eligibility, rolled quantities, and per-stack delivery state **before exposing loot**. Expose the last slot's ordinary/signature and completion inventories together through its shared loot interaction, without replaying earlier slots' signatures. This is delivery at the battle site, not automatic per-player inventory rewards. Stage and commit server claims so retrying an interrupted claim never grants the same stack twice; this needs crash-boundary verification, not just a Boolean in memory. No world-first guarantee flag exists in V5.
5. Every player sees the **same finite inventory**, with serialized server claims; no personal duplicate, kill-steal bonus, participation multiplier, offline mail, or free reroll. Any player may take remaining loot. Shared-server theft/party distribution uses normal social rules; no party system is introduced. Inventory-full claims leave the remaining stack in the same reward record. A moved dropped stack is not recreated from that record.
6. Corpse/remnant storage uses ordinary decay/loss rules, whose concrete duration must be chosen during lifecycle planning. Reload reattaches unclaimed records to the **same** saved corpse/remnant; it must never create a second container. Already claimed rewards remain claimed even after the body is gone. Cleanup/deletion cannot recreate expired loot elsewhere. Claim handling is likely custom behavior; no new reusable reward item or special UI system is required by the proposal.
7. Retreat does not reroll or reset anything. A partial group remains the same event until the approved lifecycle closes it. If despawn/absence/removal closes it without four eligible deaths, mark reward completion failed; no completion on expiry, timeout, waiting out the storm, or unload. Do not invent a group absence timer here. A later legitimate heavy storm creates a new event with fresh rewards. No historical slot deaths carry over.
8. Death/respawn or disconnect of a player does not refill reward inventories or reset encounter slots. Other players can finish/claim. Server reload restores encounter identity, slot states, ownership, attribution, outcomes and delivery states; if recovery is ambiguous, reconcile the ledger before issuing anything. This proposal does not choose storm-recovery timing or multiplayer stability-penalty scope.

Finite IDs bound rewards from traps without requiring a no-trap combat redesign. Attribution is deliberately shared; no DPS threshold or last-hit ownership decides who may loot. Event-origin guard reward suppression persists after victory even if those guards remain alive. No automatic guard cleanup or change to cap accounting is authorized by this reward proposal.

The mean is not a promised finish date. A group shares these actual items, not one per player.
